Replay Clipper recording engine: licences and source
=====================================================

The "engine" folder is a separate program from the Replay Clipper application. It consists of

  1. ReplayClipperEngine.exe (bin\64bit), a small helper written for Replay Clipper, and
  2. unmodified binaries of OBS Studio 33.0.1 and of the libraries OBS Studio ships with.

The Replay Clipper application starts the helper as a separate process and exchanges messages with
it over a named pipe (protocol: src\engine\PROTOCOL.md). The application does not link to any file in
this folder.

Obtaining the source
--------------------
Every binary above is unmodified, and the complete corresponding source is available from the
locations listed. The source of the helper is included with this program and, with the rest of
Replay Clipper, published at https://github.com/Lunafreya123/ReplayClipper . If any of the
locations above has become unavailable, the distributor of this copy of Replay Clipper will provide
the corresponding source of the binaries in this folder on request for at least three years after
distributing this copy, for no more than the cost of making the copy (open an issue at
https://github.com/Lunafreya123/ReplayClipper/issues ).
